RUNNER'S HIGH

HOW TO PLAY

  • 🏃Keep running! Your wolf runs automatically. Survive as long as you can; the speed increases with distance.
  • 🚧Jump over roadblocks. Hit one and your run ends.
  • 💵Grab cash. Every cash stack adds 100 points. Distance also adds to your score.
  • ✨Get near misses. Barely clear a roadblock for 50 bonus points.
  • 🍫Collect brownies. Carry up to 3. Use one to slow down for 6 seconds (and warp the music!).
  • 🏆Chase the “HIGH” Scores! Beat your best run and submit your initials.
